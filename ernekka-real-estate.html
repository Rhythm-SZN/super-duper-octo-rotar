What real estate services does Ernekka offer?

Ernekka's Real Estate division covers property advisory, development and related services. The appropriate scope depends on the requirements of each client.

Can I enquire about a property or development project?

Yes. You can contact the team to explain your interests, objectives and the kind of support you are looking for.

Can Nigerians living abroad make enquiries?

Yes. If you're exploring Nigerian real estate from overseas, visit our dedicated Diaspora Investment page or contact the team with your enquiry.

How do I get started?

Send an enquiry through our Contact page. Include a brief description of your property interests and what you hope to achieve so the team can understand your requirements.

Does Ernekka guarantee property investment returns?

No investment return should be assumed or treated as guaranteed. Property decisions involve risks and should be assessed against your circumstances and the relevant professional advice.
