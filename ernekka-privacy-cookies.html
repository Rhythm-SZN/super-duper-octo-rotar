2. Information we may collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with us. It may include:

Information you provide

  • Full name and contact details, such as email address and telephone number.
  • Business or organisation details you choose to provide.
  • Location or country information relevant to an enquiry.
  • Information about your property, consultancy, media or partnership enquiry.
  • Messages, documents or other information you voluntarily send us.
  • Information required to manage a client relationship or provide an agreed service.

Technical and website information

Depending on the website features and technologies actually deployed, we may process technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from network information, pages visited, referral information and website interaction data.

The website's actual analytics, logging and tracking configuration must be confirmed before publication of this statement.

Information from other sources

Where appropriate and lawful, we may receive relevant professional or business information from publicly available sources, business partners, referrals or service providers. We will explain the source where required by applicable law.

3. How we use information

We may use personal information for purposes such as:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests for information.
  • Understanding which Ernekka division or service may be relevant to your enquiry.
  • Communicating with prospective and existing clients.
  • Preparing proposals, discussing projects and managing engagements.
  • Providing agreed services and fulfilling contractual responsibilities.
  • Maintaining business, financial and administrative records.
  • Managing website security, functionality and performance.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Improving our services and communications where permitted.

We will not use personal information for an unrelated purpose without an appropriate lawful basis and, where required, informing you or obtaining your consent.

4. Our lawful basis for processing

Where applicable data protection law requires a lawful basis, the basis we rely on will depend on the specific purpose and circumstances of the processing. These may include:

  • Consent: where you have freely agreed to a specific use of your information.
  • Contract: where processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering a contract or to perform a contract.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is required to comply with applicable law.
  • Legitimate interests: where permitted and where those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.
  • Other recognised grounds: where applicable law provides another lawful basis.

We will identify the relevant lawful basis for each processing activity as part of our final privacy and data-management documentation. Consent will not be treated as the default basis for every activity.

5. When we may share information

We do not treat personal information as a commodity for sale. We may share relevant information where necessary and lawful with:

  • Authorised employees and representatives who need the information to perform their duties.
  • Professional advisers, including legal, accounting and compliance advisers.
  • Technology, hosting, communications and business-support providers engaged to support our operations.
  • Project partners or service providers where sharing is necessary for an agreed engagement and appropriately disclosed.
  • Regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies or other authorities where required or permitted by law.

Where third parties process information on our behalf, we will take appropriate steps to define their responsibilities and protect the information, as required by applicable law.

The actual providers, recipients and contractual safeguards must be documented before this notice is finalised.

6. International transfers

Ernekka may interact with clients, partners and service providers in different countries. Depending on the systems used and the location of the relevant parties, personal information may be accessed or processed across national borders.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will apply the transfer requirements and safeguards required by the data protection laws that apply to the particular transfer.

Before launch, Ernekka should identify its hosting location, form or CRM provider, email provider, analytics tools and any other services that may receive or access personal data.

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, taking account of legal, contractual, accounting and operational requirements.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the nature of the relationship. Information may be securely deleted, anonymised or otherwise disposed of when it is no longer required, subject to applicable law.

8. How we protect information

We intend to use appropriate organisational and technical measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction.

The measures used will depend on the nature of the information, the systems involved and the risks associated with processing. No online system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Ernekka should document its actual access controls, account security, backups, staff procedures, vendor safeguards and incident-response arrangements before making more specific security claims.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you and the circumstances of the processing, you may have rights that include:

  • Requesting access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Requesting deletion or erasure in circumstances where the law provides that right.
  • Requesting restriction of certain processing.
  • Objecting to certain types of processing.
  • Requesting portability of information where applicable.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Seeking human intervention or challenging certain automated decisions, where applicable.
  • Making a complaint to the relevant data protection authority.

These rights are not absolute in every circumstance. Applicable law may provide exceptions or conditions.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the approved privacy contact details listed in the contact section. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies may also be used to support website functionality, understand usage or remember preferences.

Types of cookies

Strictly necessary cookies

These support essential website functions, security, navigation or a service you have requested. They do not require the same optional-consent treatment as non-essential tracking cookies under applicable rules.

Preference cookies

These may remember choices such as language or display preferences, where such features are implemented.

Analytics cookies

If enabled, these may help us understand how visitors use the website and identify areas for improvement. They should only be activated in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

Marketing cookies

If enabled, these may be used to support advertising, campaign measurement or related activities. They should not be activated before the required consent is obtained.

Managing your preferences

Where optional cookies or similar technologies are used, the website should provide a clear way to accept, reject or manage them. You should also be able to change your preferences later.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features.

Ernekka action required

Add a current cookie inventory before publication. Confirm the names, providers, purposes, cookie lifetimes and categories for every cookie and tracking technology. Remove any category that the website does not actually use.
