Clarity matters

Know what you're agreeing to.

Before making a property commitment, prospective buyers should have the opportunity to ask questions and understand the details of the transaction.

  • Ask for relevant property and ownership documentation.
  • Understand all costs, payment arrangements and contractual obligations.
  • Clarify who is responsible for each part of the process.
  • Seek independent professional advice where appropriate.

Specific verification, legal, payment and transaction procedures must be confirmed by Ernekka before publication or reliance.

Questions, answered

A few things you may want to know.

Have a question that isn't covered here? Send us an enquiry and tell us what you'd like to understand.

Can I explore property opportunities while living abroad?

Yes. This page is intended to help prospective clients abroad begin a conversation about property interests in Nigeria. The available services and process should be confirmed with Ernekka.

Do I need to know which property I want before contacting Ernekka?

No. You can use the enquiry form to describe your interests, preferred locations and questions, even if you are still exploring.

How are property documents and ownership checked?

The specific verification process and the professionals responsible for it must be confirmed by Ernekka. Buyers should request relevant documentation and consider independent legal advice before committing.

Can I arrange a consultation in French?

The page supports English and French. Ernekka should confirm whether French-speaking consultation support is available before this is advertised as a service.

Does Ernekka guarantee investment returns?

No investment return should be assumed or treated as guaranteed. Property decisions involve risks, and prospective buyers should assess each opportunity carefully.
